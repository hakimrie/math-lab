About MathLab

MathLab is a digital mathematics laboratory, interactive textbook, and creative visual sandbox built to transform abstract mathematical concepts into tangible, living simulations.

Three Signature Explorations:

  • 1. Fractal Zoomer: Hardware-accelerated GLSL shader rendering of the Mandelbrot, Julia, and Burning Ship sets with continuous smooth coloring and infinite zoom.
  • 2. Fourier Epicycles: Freehand shape drawing decomposed via Discrete Fourier Transform into rotating complex vectors reconstructing your drawing circle by circle.
  • 3. Chaos & Double Pendulum: Physics-accurate Lagrangian double pendulum integrated via 4th-order Runge-Kutta (RK4), featuring sensitivity to initial conditions and real-time divergence plots.

Built with pure modern web technologies (HTML5, WebGL 2.0, TypeScript, Canvas 2D, KaTeX) running 100% locally in your browser with zero latency and high-performance GPU shaders.
