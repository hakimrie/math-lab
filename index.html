About MathLab

MathLab is a digital mathematics laboratory, interactive textbook, and creative visual sandbox built to transform abstract mathematical concepts into tangible, living simulations.

🌐 BukuAnak Experiments Hub
Part of the BukuAnak digital lab collection. Explore more interactive tools and creative experiments.
All Experiments

Four Signature Explorations:

  • 1. Fractal Zoomer: Hardware-accelerated GLSL shader rendering of the Mandelbrot, Julia, Burning Ship, and Tricorn sets with continuous smooth coloring and infinite zoom.
  • 2. Fourier Epicycles: Freehand shape drawing decomposed via Discrete Fourier Transform into rotating complex vectors, complete with additive Web Audio harmonic overtone synthesis.
  • 3. Chaos & Double Pendulum: Physics-accurate Lagrangian double pendulum integrated via 4th-order Runge-Kutta, featuring both Twin Butterfly Effect and a 45-pendulum undulating Silk Ribbon ensemble with live Phase Space Portrait.
  • 4. 3D Strange Attractors: Interactive 3D particle swarm and streamline flow fields exploring Lorenz, Aizawa, Halvorsen, Thomas, Rössler, and Dadras chaotic attractors with real-time 3D orbit camera.
